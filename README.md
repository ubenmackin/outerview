# OuterView

**The web, edge to edge.**

OuterView is a web browser for iPhone and iPad that fills your whole screen with the page you're reading — and gets the controls out of your way. It's designed from the ground up for the iPhone Duo's outer display, and it feels right at home on every iPhone and iPad.

## Why OuterView?

On the iPhone Duo's outer display, other browsers pin the page to a narrow strip and leave most of the screen empty. OuterView claims the entire display, edge to edge. When you unfold, everything adapts instantly — a wide layout with a top toolbar and tab strip, or a tall layout with a thumb-friendly tab bar. And on any iPhone or iPad, you get the same familiar, comfortable browsing.

## Highlights

### Controls that step aside
- Scroll down and the controls fade away — just you and the page.
- Scroll back up and everything returns right where you left it.
- Tap the address bar at any time to bring it all back.

### Made for gestures
- Swipe the address bar up to shrink the page into a live tab card.
- Swipe it left or right to flip between your tabs.
- In the tab overview, drag across the bottom picker to slide between All Tabs, Private, and your groups.

### A smarter address bar
- Instant suggestions from your open tabs, favorites, bookmarks, and history as you type.
- Live search suggestions from Google, Bing, and DuckDuckGo.
- Pick a search engine once and it's remembered.

### Tabs your way
- A visual tab overview with live page previews, search, and swipe-to-close.
- Organize tabs into named groups and switch between them with one tap.
- Private tabs never touch your history — and they're guarded by Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

### Your library, all in one place
- Favorites, Bookmarks, History, and Reading List in a single home.
- Bring your Safari bookmarks with you: export them from Safari, then import the file right from the Start Page. No accounts, no sync services.

### Reading, comfortably
- Reader mode strips away the clutter, with light, sepia, and dark themes and adjustable text size.
- Set a text size for a specific site and OuterView remembers it.
- Find on Page with a live match counter.

### Downloads that keep going
- Downloads continue in the background, even if the app is suspended.
- Finished files land in your Downloads folder, ready in the Files app.

### Private by design
- No accounts. No analytics. No telemetry. No servers. Your browsing data never leaves your device.
- A built-in content blocker stops major ad and tracking networks from loading.
- See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for the details.

## Handy to know

- **Share, save, and open**: Press and hold any link or image to open it in a new tab, add it to your Reading List, copy it, share it, or save images to your Photos.
- **Reader and zoom**: Open the page menu from the address bar to show Reader, find text on the page, or adjust the text size.
- **Start Page**: New tabs open to your favorites and recent visits, so your favorite sites are one tap away.

## Requirements

- iPhone or iPad running iOS 27.1 or later.
- The edge-to-edge outer display experience is built for the iPhone Duo.
